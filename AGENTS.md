# AI contributor guidance

Follow the [development flow](docs/development-flow.md), including its
[English collaboration convention](docs/development-flow.md#collaboration-language),
and the [review and merge guidelines](docs/review-guidelines.md).

Issue #13 is the authoritative hardware-completion tracker. Partial changes must
use plain references such as "Related to #13", report unmet acceptance criteria,
and leave it open. A successful checkpoint, green CI or intermediate merge is not
permission to close it. Do not reopen superseded Issue #11. See the
[conformance matrix](docs/hardware-conformance.md) for current evidence and the
external GitWeave closure behavior that still needs correction.
