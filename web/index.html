Help

Controls

Focus the display to use the keyboard. Player 1 uses the arrows for directions, Z/X/C for A/B/C, A/S/D for X/Y/Z, Q/W for L/R and Enter for Start. Keys use physical positions. Player 2 uses I/K/J/L for up/down/left/right, U/O/P for A/B/C and Right Shift for Start.

The first two gamepads fill free ports. Standard buttons 0–5 map to A/B/C/X/Y/Z, 7/6 to L/R, 9 to Start and 12–15 to the D-pad. On touch screens, slide across the D-pad to press diagonals and hold several buttons at once.

Local media

Insert one CHD v5 CD-ROM (cdlz/cdzl/cdfl or uncompressed), a cooked 2048-byte-sector ISO, or a CUE and all its BINARY tracks together. You can also drop them on the Disc slot. CHD and CUE track data are limited to 1,058,400,000 bytes, CUE sheets to 1 MiB, and ISO images to 18–450,000 sectors. CUE sheets may explicitly reference an SRW1 diagnostic companion. Large images need sufficient browser memory.

A valid raw 512 KiB BIOS takes precedence and can boot with or without a disc. Clear a rejected BIOS or replace it to recover. With a disc alone, the emulator uses BIOS-less HLE. Firmware and game compatibility are incomplete. Use media you are authorized to use.

Disc, BIOS and backup RAM stay in this tab. Export backup RAM in Advanced before closing or reloading.

Playback

Reset cold-boots the selected media and retains backup RAM. Help, Advanced and Debug pause playback. Select Resume when ready. If your browser blocks sound, select Enable audio.

Clean shows the original pixels. CRT Composite blends the most, S-Video keeps colors sharper, and RGB keeps the sharpest edges. CRT modes fall back to Clean when WebGL 2 is unavailable.
